Privacy Policy

App: Skylore Developer: Gihun Kim Contact: kikamui@gmail.com Effective date: October 28, 2026

Summary

This app does not collect any personal data and does not send anything to a server. All sky calculations, sensor processing, and photo capture happen entirely on your device. The developer cannot see who you are, where you are, or what you looked at.

1. Device features the app uses, and why

Feature Purpose Leaves your device?
Location (GPS) Your latitude and longitude are used to calculate which stars are visible from where you are. No. Used only for the calculation; never stored or transmitted.
Motion sensors (gyroscope, compass) To know which part of the sky the phone is pointing at. No.
Camera Only when you open Night Vision or Star Trails. No. Frames are used only for on-screen display and photo stacking.
Photo library (add-only) Only to save a star-trail photo you captured to your Photos app. No. The app does not read your existing photos and requests add-only access.

iOS asks you for each of these permissions directly. If you decline, the app's core feature (exploring the sky by dragging) still works.

2. Settings stored on your device

Only app preferences — language, sky-brightness setting, night mode — are stored locally in iOS's standard settings storage. They never leave the device and are deleted when you delete the app.

3. What we do not collect

  • Identity information such as name, email, or phone number
  • Accounts — there is no sign-up or login
  • Location history or movement
  • Usage statistics or analytics
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • Cookies or tracking technologies

4. Network use

The app works fully offline. The star catalog is bundled inside the app, and the developer operates no server. The only network communication is with Apple's App Store during an in-app purchase, handled by Apple. The app contains no advertising.

5. In-app purchase

The app offers a single one-time in-app purchase that removes the time and frequency limits on star-trail capture. Payment is handled entirely by Apple's App Store; the developer never receives or stores any payment details, name, or address. The app only learns from Apple whether the purchase was made. Refunds follow Apple's policy: https://support.apple.com/118223

6. Children's privacy

The app collects no personal data from users of any age, and therefore collects none from children.

7. Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or retained, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. You can revoke any device permission at any time in iOS Settings → [app name].

8. Changes to this policy

Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. If a future feature introduces new data processing, it will be described here and noted in the app update.
